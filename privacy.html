Ping

Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Ping is an Android app for calls, SMS and contacts. This policy explains what data Ping uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have. In short: your data stays on your phone. The only exception is an optional backup to your own Google Drive, which is encrypted before it leaves your phone.

Data on your phone

To work as your dialer, SMS and contacts app, Ping reads and writes your call log, your text messages and your phonebook contacts using Android's own storage. Ping does this only on your phone. It does not copy this data to any server.

Private contacts, and the notes, dates and ledger entries you add to them, are stored in an encrypted database inside Ping. They are not written to your phonebook, the call log, shared storage or any other app, unless you choose "Move to phonebook".

Ping may store the caller name your mobile network shows for a call (caller ID) next to the contact, on your phone only.

Optional Google Drive backup

When you first open Ping you choose either This phone only or Back up to Google Drive. You can change this later in Settings.

If you choose Google Drive backup:

  • Ping asks Google for one permission only: drive.appdata. It lets Ping use a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only Ping can see. Ping cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Before upload, Ping encrypts your private contacts on your phone (AES-256-GCM). The key is protected by a recovery passphrase that only you know. Ping never uploads or stores your passphrase, so neither Google nor the developer of Ping can read your backup.
  • The backup holds your private contacts only. Your call log, messages and phonebook stay where they are.
  • If you forget your passphrase, the backup cannot be recovered by anyone.

Ping's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to store and restore your own encrypted backup. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone.

What Ping does not do

  • No servers run by the developer. No accounts with the developer.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No cloud caller-ID or spam lookups. Numbers are not sent anywhere for identification.

Other apps you open from Ping

Buttons such as WhatsApp, Telegram, email, UPI payment and Maps open those apps with the number, address or payment ID you tapped. Ping does nothing else with them. What those apps do is covered by their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Ping deletes everything Ping stored on your phone, including private contacts. Your call log, messages and phonebook belong to Android and remain.
  • To delete your Drive backup: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → Ping → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove Ping's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Ping is not directed at children under 13.
