Ping

A private phone, messages and contacts app for Android

Ping replaces your phone's dialer, SMS and contacts apps. It keeps your data on your phone, and works fully with a D-pad on keypad phones.

What Ping does

  • Makes and receives calls, and sends and receives SMS, as your default apps.
  • Shows your phonebook contacts, and lets you edit them.
  • Keeps private contacts: people you don't want in your phonebook or in other apps. They are stored encrypted inside Ping only.
  • Optionally backs up private contacts to your own Google Drive, encrypted on your phone with your own recovery passphrase before upload.

Privacy in one line

Ping has no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone except the optional encrypted backup to your own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy.
